ABOUT

About Naija Newspaper

Naija Newspaper is a digital publication and reference directory covering Nigerian stories, people, creators, culture and public-interest topics.

Profiles and stories are editorially reviewed. A profile is an editorial reference and does not itself establish platform verification or a Google Knowledge Panel.

EDITORIAL

Corrections

We aim to correct factual errors promptly. Send the article title, the specific issue and supporting evidence to our editorial desk.
